What clients get

What working with me looks like

  1. I can work from an outcome, not just instructions.

    You can bring me a finished specification, but you can also bring me a messy spreadsheet, a reporting problem, a manual process, or an idea that still needs structure.

  2. I communicate throughout the work.

    Clients know what I am working on, where the project stands, what I need from them, and when something needs a decision.

  3. I work well inside existing teams.

    I can take ownership of my part of a project while working with founders, analysts, developers, operations teams, and other stakeholders.

  4. I think beyond the immediate task.

    A dashboard should be maintainable. A spreadsheet should still make sense next month. An automation should reduce work. A web tool should solve the problem it was built for.

  5. I am used to remote delivery.

    Much of my client work has been completed remotely through Upwork, Fiverr, and direct engagements, so async communication, handovers, revisions, documentation, and deadlines are familiar parts of the work.
